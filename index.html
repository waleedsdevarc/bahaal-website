Bahaal is a volunteer-led project started in 2024 in Gujranwala, repairing water pumps and leaks, and installing low-cost solar and biogas systems for low-income and elderly families who would otherwise go without. Every fix starts with a phone call and a name written in a notebook — and every home served makes it more likely the next family hears about us too.

What We Do

We take reports directly from the community: a widow's broken water pump, a leaking line, a kitchen with no affordable fuel, a failing electrical connection. We triage by who needs it most and what we can genuinely build or repair safely, then a small trained team goes to the home and does the work alongside the family.

We stay strictly inside what our team can do safely, and never take on licensed wiring or structural work that needs a professional tradesman.

  • Low-cost biogas units that turn household waste into cooking fuel
  • Small solar setups for basic power needs
  • Water-saving repairs on pumps and leaking lines
